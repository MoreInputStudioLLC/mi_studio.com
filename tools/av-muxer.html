⚠ You opened this as a local file, so Export won't work here — browsers block the background worker ffmpeg needs on file:// pages. Launch with Run A-V Muxer.bat (in this folder) instead; it serves the tool from localhost, where Export works. Preview & the timeline are fine either way.

Drops video + audio, lines the score up on a timeline, and bakes them into one .mp4. The video is stream-copied — never re-encoded, so picture quality is untouched. Only the audio is trimmed / offset / stretched (pitch preserved) and re-encoded.
Everything runs in your browser and works fully offline — the ffmpeg engine is bundled with the tool (in ffmpeg/); nothing is uploaded and nothing is fetched from the internet.
